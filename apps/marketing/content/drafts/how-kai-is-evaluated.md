# How K-AI 1.0 Is Evaluated

Status: blocked on verified methodology and results
Category: Research

## Required evidence

- Approved task suite, datasets, graders, baselines, dates, model versions, sampling settings, and reproducibility notes.
- Raw or reviewable results with limitations and conflict-of-interest disclosure.

Do not publish rankings or scores until this evidence exists. The article should explain the methodology before presenting any result.
