# Legal review required

This file records statements that should be reviewed by qualified counsel or the founder. Product facts were corrected in code where the repository was conclusive; uncertain legal positions were not guessed.

## Items requiring review

1. **Legal entity and address** — The policies name “Kontinue AI” but do not provide the registered legal entity, registration jurisdiction, company number, or service address.
2. **Governing law** — The Terms select California law and American Arbitration Association rules. Confirm the intended contracting entity and jurisdiction.
3. **International data transfers** — The Privacy Policy says the service is provided in the United States. Confirm every current hosting, storage, analytics, authentication, extraction, and AI-provider location before retaining this statement.
4. **Retention periods** — The policy uses “as long as necessary” but the product does not expose a verified deletion schedule for chats, account data, import jobs, logs, analytics, backups, or error monitoring.
5. **Account deletion** — The policy permits deletion requests by contact, but no self-service account-deletion route was found. Confirm the operational process, identity verification, provider deletion, backup handling, and response deadline.
6. **AI training statement** — The policy says personal conversations and imported chat data are not used to train AI models. Confirm this contractually across every external model, routing, extraction, observability, and subprocessor path.
7. **Subprocessor list** — Vercel, Clerk, Convex, Sentry, Sanity, Resend, R2-compatible storage, Firecrawl, model gateways/providers, and other services appear in the repository. Counsel should decide which must be disclosed and how.
8. **Cookie notice and consent** — No dedicated cookie notice or consent-management implementation was found. Determine requirements by visitor location and the actual GTM/analytics configuration.
9. **Refund wording** — The Terms state no refunds for partial months or unused services. Confirm alignment with Clerk billing settings, consumer-protection law, chargeback handling, trials, and any discretionary refund practice.
10. **Plan changes** — The Terms now name Starter ($8.99/month) and Pro ($50/month) to match repository configuration. Confirm whether prices belong in the legal document or should instead be incorporated by reference to checkout.
11. **Age and children** — The Terms permit users aged 13+ with guardian permission under 18. Confirm that the product, processors, and target jurisdictions support this policy.
12. **Imported third-party content** — Confirm the user warranty, licence, privacy obligations, and platform-terms implications for importing public shared links and account export files.
13. **Connected services** — Verify every listed Google, GitHub, Notion, Vercel, and Todoist capability, scope, revocation flow, and Limited Use statement against production configuration.
